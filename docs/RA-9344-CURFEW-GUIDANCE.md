# Curfew Violation Operational Guidance

This document describes CivicGuard fields and workflow considerations for recording curfew-related incidents involving minors. It is an operational aid, not legal advice or a substitute for the current text of Republic Act No. 9344, its amendments, implementing rules, or barangay policy.

## Recording Principles

- Record only information necessary for the incident record and safeguard the minor's privacy.
- Use the minor, guardian, apprehension, prior-violation, and notes fields to document what was observed and what action was taken.
- Record whether a guardian was notified and the referral or follow-up action selected.
- Avoid language that labels or criminalizes the child. Describe observed facts and the assistance or referral provided.
- Do not publish identifying information in public reports or screenshots.
- Escalate safeguarding concerns to the appropriate barangay or social-welfare personnel according to current local procedures.

## Required Operational Review

Before deployment, barangay officials should confirm the local process for guardian notification, referral to social-welfare services, documentation access, retention, and authorized follow-up. The system fields support documentation but do not determine the legally appropriate intervention.
